Before you move, look for checks, captures, and threats for both sides. Material helps you find a winning plan, but it is not a draw or timeout tiebreaker.

REFLECT · LEARN FROM EVERY GAME

Every game has a lesson.

Analyze the turning points: what happened, what the strongest reply was, and which move could have changed the result.

♟ Material helps you plan; it does not break a draw.

In a timed game, running out of time usually loses unless the opponent cannot possibly checkmate. A drawn game is not decided by counting pieces. Use material and passed pawns to find a practical winning plan while the game is still live.
