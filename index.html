A note about the goal

This training path combines tactics, calculation, positional play, endgames, opening work, slow games, PGN review, and full-strength Stockfish analysis. The engine can expose mistakes and test your ideas; it cannot replace tournament play, expert coaching, or a deliberate long-term study plan, and no app can promise 2800 Elo.
